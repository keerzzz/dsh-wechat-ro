# 微信历史来源

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing DSH sidebar and daily cards. Keep its dark/light alias tokens, native typeface, controls and navigation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A user can identify the source, read the content, and inspect its original record without sorting mixed conversation lists.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep the daily overview, highlights and history sources as three tabs. The source view has separate Moments, mini-program, favorites, contacts and voice tabs, with a count and clear reading scope.

INTERACTION: Switching a source replaces the current list. Search, paging and selected voice transcription operate on that source. Arrow keys move through tabs. References expand beneath the record they support.

TYPE AND MATERIAL: Reuse DSH label, background and border aliases. Separate author, Beijing time, body and citation with spacing and weight. Wrap long content and identifiers inside the sidebar.

RESPONSIVE: Preserve readable content and usable controls in narrow panels. Allow source tabs to wrap. Do not redesign DSH's surrounding application shell.

STATES: Show loading, empty and failed reads near the affected list. Display previous daily results during refresh failures. Label voice transcripts as derived text. Selected model vision remains an explicit control.
